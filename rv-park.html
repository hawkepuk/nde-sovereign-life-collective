OUR NONPROFIT PURPOSE

More than a place to stay.

The guest accommodations envisioned for this project are part of a broader nonprofit vision centered on cultural learning, family wellness, traditional martial arts and responsible land stewardship.

If visitor services open in the future, NSLC intends to structure them to support the organization's mission and the long-term care of the property. Details about fees, program access and allocation of any operating proceeds will depend on board decisions, financial feasibility and applicable nonprofit rules.

The project is not taking reservations or charging for on-site services. Read our progress updates to follow developments as they can be confirmed.
