Nde RV Park & Wellness Center

A land‑based development rooted in Apache values, movement, healing, and community strength.

Project Overview

The Nde RV Park & Wellness Center is a hybrid Indigenous‑led development combining land stewardship, family‑friendly RV accommodations, and a wellness‑focused dojang for Taekwon‑Do and movement‑based healing. Designed for travelers, families, and community members, this center provides safe, culturally grounded spaces for rest, training, and connection. Currently under development, it aims to open its doors to the community in the near future.

RV Park Features

  • 10-15 full‑hookup RV spaces
  • Pet‑friendly walking paths
  • Family‑friendly recreation areas
  • Secure, well‑lit grounds with camera coverage
  • Access to wellness programs and dojang

Wellness Center & Dojang

The wellness center includes a dedicated Taekwon‑Do dojang, movement studio, and community gathering space. Programs focus on Indigenous wellness, martial arts, family fitness, and cultural identity.

Land‑Based Development Vision

This project is part of a long‑term plan to restore Indigenous land relationships, create sustainable rural infrastructure, and build a safe, culturally grounded wellness campus for future generations.
