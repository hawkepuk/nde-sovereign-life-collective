SPOKANE COUNTY · COMMUNITY DIRECTORY

Help is closer
than you think.

A starting point for individuals and families looking for food, housing, financial assistance, family support, counseling and other community services.

Need help finding the right service?

Call 2-1-1 for referrals to local health and human services. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. For mental health or suicide crisis support, call or text 988.

Before you visit

Programs, addresses, phone numbers, hours, funding and eligibility can change. This directory is a starting point—not a guarantee of services, funding, placement or immediate availability. Please contact each organization to confirm details, and let us know if a listing needs correction.
