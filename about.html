WHO WE ARE

About NDE
Sovereign Life Collective.

Indigenous-led. Community-centered. Guided by Chiricahua Apache values and a commitment to future generations.

OUR STORY

Built on respect.
Moving toward the future.

NDE Sovereign Life Collective brings people together around culture, learning, wellness and service.

As an Indigenous-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit, NSLC is building organizational capacity and developing charitable initiatives intended to strengthen families, support youth, encourage cultural learning and promote responsible relationships with the land.

Our work is guided by Chiricahua Apache values of respect, responsibility, resilience and care for future generations. We believe meaningful community development begins by listening, honoring identity and creating opportunities for people to grow with dignity.

Organizational milestones: NSLC was established on August 4, 2026. Its Washington State charitable organization registration was filed on August 17, 2026 (State Registration No. 2013537). NSLC reports receiving federal 501(c)(3) tax-exempt recognition on September 11, 2026 and obtaining its Washington business license on October 2, 2026. Federal EIN: 42-4286493. WHY WE EXIST

Our mission.

Our mission and vision are the foundation of who we are and the reason our work exists.

MISSION STATEMENT

To strengthen Indigenous families and communities through culturally grounded wellness programs, martial arts instruction and thoughtful land-based development.

We work toward safe, supportive spaces where people can build confidence, connection, wellbeing and self-determination.

WHERE WE ARE GOING

Our vision.

VISION STATEMENT

We envision spaces where movement, identity and healing meet—building resilience, confidence and self-determination.

We see a future where Indigenous identity is respected, families have access to meaningful wellness and learning opportunities, young people are supported, and land is cared for as a living legacy for generations to come.

FROM PURPOSE TO PRACTICE

How we put our mission into action.

Our charitable priorities connect cultural education, family wellbeing, youth development and responsible stewardship.

01 · CULTURAL KNOWLEDGE

Learning & connection

Support Indigenous cultural education and respectful opportunities to share traditions, histories and living community perspectives.

02 · FAMILY WELLNESS

Movement & strength

Develop family-friendly Taekwon-Do, wellness and youth initiatives that encourage confidence, discipline, belonging and resilience.

03 · LAND STEWARDSHIP

A living legacy

Plan a wellness campus and future community spaces that place conservation, thoughtful land use and community benefit alongside one another.
