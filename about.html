THE COLLECTIVE

About NSLC

Indigenous-led. Community-centered. Guided by Chiricahua Apache cultural values and a commitment to future generations.

OUR STORY

Built on respect.
Moving toward the future.

NDE Sovereign Life Collective brings people together around culture, learning, wellness and service.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, NSLC is developing programs and spaces that strengthen families, build confidence and support Indigenous-led community development.

Our RV Park & Wellness Center initiative brings together cultural education, family wellness, responsible land planning and conservation. Architectural images illustrate the vision; guest operations have not begun.

We share organization news and upcoming milestones on our Progress & Updates page.

VALUES THAT GUIDE US

The principles beneath every plan.

01

Respect

For our people, the land, our relationships and the right to tell our own stories.

02

Sovereignty

Supporting identity, self-determination and Indigenous-led decisions.

03

Strength

Building resilience through disciplined movement, wellness and mutual care.

04

Stewardship

Planning responsibly so the land remains a source of life and learning.
