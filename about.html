About Us

Indigenous‑led. Community‑driven. Rooted in Chiricahua Apache values and wellness traditions.

Who We Are

Nde Sovereign Life Collective is an Indigenous‑led organization guided by Chiricahua Apache cultural principles, community wellness, and land‑based practices. We work to strengthen families, empower youth, and build spaces where Indigenous identity, movement, and healing come together.

What We Do

Our programs blend traditional values with modern community needs. Through martial arts instruction, wellness initiatives, rural development planning, and cultural education, we create supportive environments where individuals and families can grow, heal, and thrive.

Our Vision

We envision a future where Indigenous communities have access to safe spaces, culturally grounded wellness programs, and land‑based healing opportunities. Our long‑term development includes creating a hybrid wellness campus and dojang that serves families, travelers, and community members with dignity and respect.

Our Values

  • Respect — Honoring land, ancestors, and community relationships.
  • Strength — Building resilience through movement, culture, and discipline.
  • Sovereignty — Supporting Indigenous self‑determination and identity.
  • Community — Creating safe, supportive spaces for families and youth.
  • Wellness — Promoting balance through mind‑body‑spirit practices.
