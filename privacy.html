Privacy & Site Information

What you should know about how this informational website works.

WEBSITE INFORMATION

Information collected by this website

This version of the NSLC website does not contain a direct online contact form, payment form or account registration. We do not intentionally collect or store personal information through these pages.

When you contact us

When you email or call NSLC, you voluntarily provide contact details and any information you include. We use these communications to respond to your inquiry and manage organizational relationships.

Third-party websites

Donation links direct you to Zeffy and GoFundMe. Their own privacy policies, payment systems and terms govern information submitted through those websites. Social media links likewise take you to third-party platforms.

Hosting and technical data

The website is designed for static hosting on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, in accordance with its policies. This website does not install its own analytics or advertising trackers.

External resources

The website uses locally served images, fonts and scripts, and does not rely on external tracking pixels or third-party web fonts. Linked external sites may have different practices.

Concept imagery

All project renderings and dimensions are illustrative. They are not representations of an acquired property or construction approvals. Historical-inspired artwork is labeled separately from documentary or archival photography.

Questions

For questions about this website or your communications with NSLC, email hawkepuk@gmail.com.

This page is a draft informational privacy notice and should receive legal review before publication if NSLC introduces analytics, donation embeds, online forms, or other data collection.
