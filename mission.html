OUR NORTH STAR

Our Mission

Creating safe, supportive spaces where identity, movement, wellness and connection with the land help communities thrive.

PROPOSED · IN DEVELOPMENT
OUR PURPOSE

To strengthen Indigenous families and communities through culturally grounded wellness programs, martial arts instruction and thoughtful land-based development.

We envision spaces where movement, identity and healing meet—building resilience, confidence and self-determination.

FROM WORDS TO ACTION

A mission with many pathways.

CULTURAL KNOWLEDGE

Learning & connection

Support Indigenous cultural education and respectful opportunities to share traditions and stories.

FAMILY WELLNESS

Movement & strength

Develop family-friendly Taekwon-Do training and programs that build confidence and belonging.

LAND STEWARDSHIP

A living legacy

Plan a wellness campus that places conservation and responsible land use alongside community benefits.

OUR CURRENT STAGE

Building with integrity.

NSLC's campus and several program areas are in planning and fundraising, not active operations.

We are working toward appropriate land acquisition, local approvals, sustainable infrastructure and the partnerships required to deliver on this mission.
