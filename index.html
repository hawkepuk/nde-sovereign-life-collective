WHO WE ARE

Rooted in identity.
Built for belonging.

NDE Sovereign Life Collective is an Indigenous-led 501(c)(3) nonprofit developing spaces and programs where culture, wellness, education and community care come together.

Our work draws on Chiricahua Apache values of respect, responsibility and resilience. We are building a long-term vision—one founded on intentional stewardship, not rapid development.
