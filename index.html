Welcome

Nde Sovereign Life Collective is an Indigenous‑led nonprofit dedicated to strengthening Indigenous identity, supporting families, and creating sustainable community programs rooted in sovereignty and wellness. Although, we are still in the development stage, our commitment to fostering Indigenous sovereignty and community wellness remains unwavering.
