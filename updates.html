PARTNERSHIPS & FUNDING

Building practical support

NSLC welcomes conversations with prospective funders, educators, volunteers, technical advisers and community partners. Funding priorities emphasize charitable purposes, while guest-only or commercial facilities must be evaluated separately for eligibility.

COMMUNITY & LAND PLANNING

Connecting purpose and place

Our planning considers cultural education, traditional Taekwon-Do, family wellness, wildlife habitat and low-impact land stewardship. A suitable property, sound engineering, permits and financial support would be required before opening facilities.

PROJECT ROADMAP

What comes next.

The NDE RV Park & Wellness Center remains a capital-project goal. The roadmap below lists priorities, not completed facilities or guaranteed dates.

PRIORITY 01 · FUNDING & PARTNERSHIPS

Build the support network

Pursue appropriate grants, community support, professional partners and responsible financial planning for charitable components.

PRIORITY 02 · LAND & FEASIBILITY

Evaluate the right property

Identify a suitable site; complete due diligence, ecological review, infrastructure assessments and required local approvals before any construction commitment.

PRIORITY 03 · IMPLEMENTATION

Develop facilities and programs

Advance the dojang, community learning areas, guest amenities and conservation plans in phases if land, financing and permits allow.

Progress reporting: Completed items above are organizational or website milestones, not claims that NSLC has acquired land, received specific grants, served a stated number of participants or opened the RV Park & Wellness Center. We will publish those outcomes only after they can be confirmed.
