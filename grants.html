GROWTH THROUGH PARTNERSHIP

Grants & Partnerships

We are seeking mission-aligned philanthropic, public and community partners to help bring a sustainable Indigenous-led campus to life.

PROPOSED · IN DEVELOPMENT
THE CASE FOR SUPPORT

Investing in people.
Stewarding the land.

NDE Sovereign Life Collective is pursuing grants and philanthropy for land acquisition, community-focused facilities, cultural education, wellness initiatives and nature stewardship.

We envision a 60–80-acre site with an approximately equal split between the wellness campus and conserved/natural open space. The concept includes ten RV sites and a separate proposed Event Center.

Applications will distinguish grant-eligible charitable components from revenue-producing or guest-restricted facilities, and will comply with each funder's restrictions.

POTENTIAL PARTNERSHIP AREAS

A role for every kind of ally.

01 / LAND

Property acquisition

Support responsible acquisition, feasibility, land stewardship planning and due diligence.

02 / COMMUNITY

Cultural & wellness spaces

Advance educational space, family activities, traditional martial arts instruction and shared community infrastructure.

03 / NATURE

Environmental stewardship

Support habitat assessment, low-impact trails, wildlife corridors and learning opportunities.

04 / INFRASTRUCTURE

Essential utilities

Explore eligible funding for drinking water, wastewater treatment, access, energy efficiency and fire safety.

05 / PROGRAMS

Youth & cultural education

Help strengthen culturally grounded programming, wellness learning and Indigenous-led initiatives.

06 / PARTNERSHIPS

In-kind expertise

Professional support for planning, land assessment, engineering, nonprofit capacity and accessible design.
